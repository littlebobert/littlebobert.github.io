Karui

Reveal unsent messages, and more.

An iPhone and iPad app that builds on Apple’s Focus modes, filtering every app’s notifications privately with Apple Intelligence. It silences spam and lets important notifications through.

Shadan

Hide the memes you’re tired of seeing.

A Safari extension for Mac. It hides look-alikes too, even in video previews, and everything runs on your Mac.

Kehai

Tame your windows and switch apps faster.

Switching apps is easy. Finding the right window isn’t. Kehai is a Mac app that finds windows and Safari tabs faster, grouped by project or task.

Visit kehai.jp
Sasu

Sasu

On-screen guidance for living abroad.

A Mac app for understanding foreign language websites, forms and apps without browser translation breaking the page. Translate to and from any language from any app.

Visit sasu.jp

Autofill everything, privately.

An intelligent Safari form filler for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Fill a few forms by hand and Umete remembers your answers, then fills in the next one for you.
