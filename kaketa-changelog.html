Kaketa

Each TestFlight build’s notes, newest first. Builds that reached the App Store carry a badge.

0.1.0 (16)

October 10, 2026
  • The check now marks the strokes it minds on the grid: missing, extra, or off, each by its number.

0.1.0 (14)

October 10, 2026
  • Fixed a bug where the Practice tab wouldn’t scroll when the swipe started on the kanji grid.
  • Fixed a bug on iPad where the "End this practice?" confirmation appeared under the window controls.

0.1.0 (13)

October 10, 2026
  • The handwriting check now runs on your device: when you show the answer, each stroke is compared with the standard stroke order, with a line per character and marks on strokes that are missing or drawn backwards.
  • Set the check's strictness, Strict, Standard, or Loose, in Settings or on the answer itself.
  • The OpenAI check is gone. Nothing leaves your device.
  • A stroke guide while writing: full, partial (the default, one stroke when you ask), or none.
  • Press a kanji tile to peek at its stroke order; drag across tiles to scrub.
  • Long words keep three squares on screen and scroll as you write.
  • A notebook look: a kanji workbook grid, highlighter chips, a seal on mastered words, and a daily tally and streak.

0.1.0 (12)

October 10, 2026
  • After a reveal, your stroke numbers no longer sit on top of the answer's numbers or on each other.

0.1.0 (11)

October 10, 2026
  • Undo takes back your last stroke while writing.
