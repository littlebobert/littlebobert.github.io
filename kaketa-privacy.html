Privacy Policy

Kaketa

Overview

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Kaketa has no account and no analytics. Your practice progress and the words you add stay on your device and in your own iCloud. The handwriting check compares your strokes with the standard ones on your device. Nothing leaves your device.

Data the app handles

  • Which words you have marked known or learning, and the review schedule and counts for each.
  • The words you add under My Words.
  • Your settings, such as the prompt and the meaning language.
  • If you turn on the attempt log: a picture of each word you wrote, with its verdict and your decision.

What you draw on the practice grid is kept only for the current card and is discarded when you move on, unless the attempt log is on.

Storage and sync

Progress, My Words, and settings are stored on your device. Progress and My Words also sync through iCloud, in your private iCloud database, between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple handles that storage under its own terms; we cannot read it.

Attempt log (optional)

Off unless you turn it on in Settings. With it on, Kaketa keeps a record of each word you write: the picture of your strokes, the word, the check’s verdict, and whether you chose Got It, Not Yet, or already mastered. The record is stored on your device and in your private iCloud database, like your progress, and is shown on each word’s page. It is not sent anywhere. You can delete it all at any time in Settings → Attempt Log.

Handwriting check

The check runs on your device: when you show the answer, Kaketa compares each stroke you drew with the standard strokes of each kanji and tells you whether the word looks right, is close, or isn’t quite there. You can set how strict it is in Settings. Nothing is sent anywhere for this. Earlier versions could send a picture of your writing to OpenAI for a second opinion; that feature has been removed.

No data collection, sharing, or tracking by the app

Kaketa does not transmit app data to us, collect it on a server, share it with third parties, or track you across apps and websites. The app sends no analytics to us. Apple gives us aggregated App Store statistics, and, if you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, aggregated usage figures and crash reports; these come from Apple and do not identify you. The kaketa.jp website does not track which links you click.
