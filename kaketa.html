See the reading, write the kanji, check the stroke order. Mark each word got it, not yet, or already known.

Words to practice

  • By school grade: the kanji taught in each year of Japanese elementary school, with common words for each, a few kanji per section like a textbook
  • By JLPT level, N5 to N1
  • My Words: your address, your family’s names, phrases from a letter you need to write by hand
  • Mark whole sections or groups as known, or select words one by one

Also:

  • Prompts you choose: the reading, the meaning, or an example sentence with the word blanked out. Switch on any card
  • Meanings in English, Japanese, or Tagalog
  • Every kanji’s stroke order, animated, on the answer
  • Optional handwriting check: with your own OpenAI API key, Kaketa asks OpenAI whether what you wrote matches
  • Progress syncs between iPhone and iPad through iCloud

Privacy:

Kaketa has no account, no server, and no analytics. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a picture of your handwriting, sent to OpenAI only when you turn the handwriting check on and tap Check. Details.
