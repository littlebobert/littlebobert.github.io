Write the word, show the answer, and see every stroke checked.

Press a kanji to see its stroke order and how its words are going.

Mark words you already know. The rest resurface when you’re about to forget them.

Practice words:

  • By school grade: the kanji taught in each year of Japanese elementary school, with common words for each
  • By JLPT level: N5 to N1
  • My Words: your address, your family’s names, phrases from a letter you need to write by hand

Also:

  • Checks each stroke you write against the standard stroke order, on your device
  • Progress syncs between iPhone and iPad
  • Meanings in English, Japanese, or Tagalog
  • Quickly mark whole sections or groups as mastered
  • Customize your prompt: the reading, the meaning, or an example sentence

Privacy:

Kaketa has no account and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud, and the handwriting check runs on your device too. Nothing leaves your device. Details.
