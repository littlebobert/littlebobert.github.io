0.1.0 (30)

September 28, 2026
  • Second Opinion has a new When to Ask setting. Choose Always to have Private Cloud Compute check every notification the on-device model sorts: Karui alerts you right away, then corrects it, and removes an alert Private Cloud Compute says wasn't needed.
  • Messages that @mention someone else, like a question to a coworker in a Slack channel, are no longer marked Important.
  • Daily summaries are now shorter.
  • Fixed some minor bugs and made some small UI improvements.

0.1.0 (29)

September 27, 2026
  • Auto Reply can now turn itself on during events on the calendars you choose, and off when they end.
  • Karui now rechecks notifications Apple Intelligence was too busy for in the background, without you opening the app.

0.1.0 (28)

September 26, 2026
  • Added Second Opinion, off by default. When iOS is too busy to run the on-device model, your Shortcut can ask Apple's Private Cloud Compute instead, and Karui alerts you if the answer is Important. Turn it on in Settings → Second Opinion, then update your Karui Shortcut when prompted.

0.1.0 (27)

September 26, 2026
  • Added Auto Reply. While it's on, Karui replies to your VIPs in Messages, WhatsApp, LINE, and Messenger with a message you choose, like "I'm driving" or "I'm asleep." It needs the latest Karui Shortcut.
  • Added quiet apps, for apps where spam tends to sound personal. Only VIPs, codes, and urgent messages alert you.
  • Settings are reorganized into People, Apps, and Sorting & Alerts.
  • Tap a VIP to see exactly what Karui saved from their contact card, or swipe to remove them.
  • Adding important and quiet apps is easier, with suggestions for common apps and for apps in your History.
  • Tapping a one-time code notification copies the code instead of opening the app it came from.
  • Karui is better at telling when a message is about one of your calendar events.
  • Fixed a bug where Karui's notifications stayed in Notification Center after you opened them in Karui.
  • Fixed a bug where a daily summary could be sent twice.
  • If you have two copies of the Karui Shortcut, Karui now sends one alert instead of two and tells you to delete the older one.

0.1.0 (26)

September 25, 2026
  • Karui is now iOS 27 only since the required Shortcut notification automation is iOS 27 only.

0.1.0 (24)

September 25, 2026
  • Fixed a bug where some email receipts would appear as one-time codes.
  • Fixed a bug where going backward in the setup wizard would make it impossible to continue.

0.1.0 (23)

September 25, 2026
  • Added a cooldown for busy conversations. After Karui alerts you about a conversation, it waits 10 minutes before alerting you about it again.
  • Important apps can be marked as work apps, which only count as important during your work hours (Mon–Fri, 9 AM to 6 PM by default).
  • Arriving rides and deliveries, and notifications an app marks as time sensitive, are now more likely to be Important.
  • Added a Copy Code button for one-time codes in History.

0.1.0 (22)

September 25, 2026
  • Added sleep hours to Wake Me. Alarms now only ring between bedtime and wake-up, 10 PM to 6 AM by default.
  • History now shows your latest daily summary, so you can reopen it after the notification is gone.
  • The daily summary's Worth a Look now includes more notifications.
  • Promote this Sender now lets you pick the person from your contacts.
  • Karui now recognizes VIPs by their contact nickname.
