Changelog

Karui

0.1.0 (26)

September 25, 2026
  • Karui is now iOS 27 only since the required Shortcut notification automation is iOS 27 only.

0.1.0 (24)

September 25, 2026
  • Fixed a bug where some email receipts would appear as one-time codes.
  • Fixed a bug where going backward in the setup wizard would make it impossible to continue.

0.1.0 (23)

September 25, 2026
  • Added a cooldown for busy conversations. After Karui alerts you about a conversation, it waits 10 minutes before alerting you about it again.
  • Important apps can be marked as work apps, which only count as important during your work hours (Mon–Fri, 9 AM to 6 PM by default).
  • Arriving rides and deliveries, and notifications an app marks as time sensitive, are now more likely to be Important.
  • Added a Copy Code button for one-time codes in History.

0.1.0 (22)

September 25, 2026
  • Added sleep hours to Wake Me. Alarms now only ring between bedtime and wake-up, 10 PM to 6 AM by default.
  • History now shows your latest daily summary, so you can reopen it after the notification is gone.
  • The daily summary's Worth a Look now includes more notifications.
  • Promote this Sender now lets you pick the person from your contacts.
  • Karui now recognizes VIPs by their contact nickname.

0.1.0 (21)

September 23, 2026
  • Improved on-device categorization.

0.1.0 (20)

September 23, 2026
  • Added a "Missed calls ring immediately" setting for Wake Me.
  • A second missed call from a Wake Me contact now rings an alarm.
  • Added a "Check My Shortcut" diagnostic. It needs the updated Karui Shortcut, which you can add again from Settings → Help & Troubleshooting or the Support Guide.
  • Fixed taps firing while scrolling on Test It.

0.1.0 (19)

September 23, 2026
  • Added Wake Me: people you choose can ring an alarm in an emergency, even when your iPhone is silenced or in a Focus.
  • Added Combine Rapid Messages: several messages in a row from the same sender now arrive as one notification.
  • Improved the daily summary: it now highlights the notifications worth a look.

0.1.0 (18)

September 22, 2026
  • Karui now rechecks notifications that Apple Intelligence was too busy to evaluate, and tells you if any were Important.
  • Added filtering in the history.

0.1.0 (17)

September 22, 2026
  • Added support for more apps.

0.1.0 (16)

September 22, 2026
  • Added repeat sender breakthroughs.
  • Added a daily digest for neutral notifications.
  • Added contact avatars for VIPs.
  • Added one-time code detection.
  • Added high performance filtering and grouping for list views.
