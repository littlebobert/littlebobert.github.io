How to See Unsent Messages on iPhone

Karui

What happens when someone unsends a message in Messages, LINE, Instagram, or WhatsApp, why you can’t get it back afterward, and how to keep a copy of messages as they arrive.

Updated November 21, 2026 · iOS 27

The short answer

Once someone unsends a message, it’s gone from the conversation, and no setting in iOS or in apps like Messages or LINE brings it back. The only way to read it is to have kept a copy before it was unsent. If its notification showed the message text, an app like Karui, which keeps a history of your notifications, can show it to you afterward.

What unsending does

  • In Messages, someone can unsend an iMessage for a couple of minutes after sending it. It disappears from both sides of the conversation, and you see that a message was unsent.
  • In LINE, the sender can unsend a message within 24 hours. It’s removed for everyone, leaving a note that a message was unsent.
  • Instagram, Messenger, and WhatsApp’s Delete for Everyone work the same way: the message is removed for everyone in the chat.

The notification you got when the message arrived can be deleted when the message is unsent.

How Karui keeps unsent messages

A Shortcuts automation hands each notification to Karui as it arrives, so if the sender unsends the message later, its text is still in Karui’s History.

  1. Install Karui from the App Store. It’s free, with no account.
  2. Open it and follow the setup. If you only want to keep unsent messages, without filtering notifications, choose to just keep unsent messages.
  3. Make sure the apps you use, like Messages, LINE, or Instagram, are in the Karui Shortcut’s list of apps.
  4. When an app says someone unsent a message, open Karui’s History to read it.

Keeping unsent messages works without Apple Intelligence. You need iOS or iPadOS 27 or later.

When it can’t help

  • Messages unsent before you set up Karui. It only keeps notifications that arrive after setup.
  • Notifications without the message text: if previews are off in Settings > Notifications > Show Previews, or the app hides message content in its notifications. In LINE, check that its notification settings show message content (メッセージ内容表示).
  • Muted chats and apps with notifications turned off, which send no notification at all.
  • A message unsent before its notification arrived.
  • Apps that aren’t in the Karui Shortcut’s list of apps.

Privacy

Karui only keeps what your iPhone already received as a notification, and it stays on your iPhone: Karui has no server, needs no account, and sends no analytics. The sender won’t be told that you read it.

Questions

Can I see an unsent message without an app?

Sometimes. If the notification is still in Notification Center, it may still show the text. Once it’s cleared, or the app removes it, iOS keeps no copy.

Does it work with LINE?

Yes, as long as LINE is in the Karui Shortcut’s list of apps and LINE’s notifications show message content.

Can I get back messages unsent before I installed Karui?

No. Karui can only keep notifications that arrive after it’s set up.
