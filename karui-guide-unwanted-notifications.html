How to Stop Unwanted Notifications on iPhone

Karui

Every way iOS lets you cut down unwanted and spam notifications, and when each one helps.

Updated November 23, 2026 · iOS 27

The short answer

Turn off notifications from apps you never need, move the rest into a Scheduled Summary, and use a Focus so only the people and apps you choose can interrupt you. Some apps send you both important and unimportant notifications, like a delivery app that also sends promotions. For that, turn on Intelligent Breakthrough & Silencing in your Focus. On top of that, Karui for iPhone also learns from you when you correct it.

1. Turn off notifications from an app

  1. Open Settings, tap Notifications, then tap the app.
  2. Turn off Allow Notifications to stop them completely. Or leave them on and turn off Sounds and Banners, so they only collect in Notification Center.

2. Turn off promotions in the app itself

Many apps let you choose which kinds of notifications they send. Look in the app’s own settings for options like promotions, recommendations, or news.

3. Batch the rest with a Scheduled Summary

In Settings > Notifications > Scheduled Summary, choose apps whose notifications can wait. They arrive together at the times you set, instead of one by one. Time-sensitive notifications can still arrive right away.

4. Use a Focus to choose who can interrupt you

A Focus, in Settings > Focus or Control Center, silences everything except the people and apps you allow. Silenced notifications still go to Notification Center, so nothing is lost.

  • Under People, choose who can reach you. Under Apps, choose which apps can.
  • If apps you didn’t allow keep getting through, turn off Time Sensitive Notifications under Apps. Apps can mark notifications as time sensitive to break through a Focus.

5. Let Apple Intelligence help

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, iOS can summarize notifications, and the Reduce Interruptions Focus shows only the notifications that seem to need your attention.

Any Focus can also use Intelligent Breakthrough & Silencing. Apple Intelligence then lets an important notification through even from an app you didn’t allow, and silences an unimportant one even from an app you did. So a delivery app’s “Your driver is here” can reach you while its “20% off this weekend” stays quiet.

Both are a good place to start. Apple Intelligence decides by itself which notifications matter, so check Notification Center now and then for anything it held back.

6. Filter spam texts and calls

  • In Settings > Apps > Messages, turn on the option to filter or screen messages from unknown senders.
  • In Settings > Apps > Phone, turn on Silence Unknown Callers or call screening to stop calls from numbers you don’t know.

These cover texts and calls. Notifications from other apps need the steps above.

What Karui adds

Your Focus silences the apps, and a Shortcuts automation passes each of their notifications to Karui, which decides with Apple Intelligence whether it’s Important, Neutral, or Spam.

  • Important notifications, like messages from friends, deliveries, and one-time codes, get through.

Questions

Why do I still get notifications during a Focus?

People and apps you allowed can still reach you, and so can apps that mark notifications as time sensitive. Check the Focus’s People and Apps settings, and turn off Time Sensitive Notifications there. If Intelligent Breakthrough & Silencing is on, Apple Intelligence can also let through notifications it thinks are important.

Will I miss the notifications I silence?

No. Silenced and summarized notifications still go to Notification Center. Karui also keeps the ones it categorizes in its History.

Does Karui work on older iPhones?

Karui needs iOS 27 or later. Spam filtering also needs Apple Intelligence, which starts with iPhone 15 Pro. Keeping a history of your notifications, including messages people unsend, works without it.
