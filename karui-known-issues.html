Known Issues

Karui

Known problems and limitations in Karui, and what you can do about them.

Updated November 23, 2026 · Karui 1.1.0

Calls in LINE and some other apps don’t ring during a Focus

Even when your Focus is set to allow calls from your iOS VIPs and you’ve added important contacts’ LINE numbers as iOS VIPs, LINE doesn’t ring through the Focus.

If you need calls in an app like this to ring, allow the app in your Focus, in iOS Settings > Focus > the Focus you use > Apps. Unfortunately its other potentially unimportant notifications then come through too. I am actively tracking this issue and looking for a workaround.

Tapping a Karui notification only opens the app, not the correct place in the app

  • Spark, Slack, and Messages will generally work correctly if you install Karui for Mac on an always-on Mac.
  • Messages and WhatsApp, without a Mac: Karui should open the conversation when it knows the sender’s phone number or email address, from the notification or a VIP’s contact card. WhatsApp group chats will only open the app, though.
  • For every other app: tapping a Karui notification simply opens the app. To go straight to the message, tap the app’s original notification in Notification Center instead. Your Focus silences it, but it’s still there.
