Known Issues

Karui

Problems we know about in Karui, and what you can do about them.

Updated November 23, 2026 · Karui 1.1.0

Calls in LINE and some other apps don’t ring during a Focus

Karui works by having your Focus silence apps, then letting their important notifications through as its own. Calls are different: when a Focus silences a messaging app that also makes calls, like LINE, its incoming calls don’t ring either, and Karui can’t make them ring. It only hears about a call from the app’s notification, often once it has already been missed.

  • If you need calls in an app like this to ring, allow the app in your Focus, in iOS Settings > Focus > the Focus you use > Apps. Its other notifications then come through as the app sends them, too.
  • Phone calls can ring through a Focus from people you choose: see Calls from VIPs don’t ring in Support.

Tapping a Karui notification usually opens the app, not the exact message

A Karui notification is a new one, not the app’s own, and iOS doesn’t give Karui the original’s link. So tapping it usually opens the app the notification came from, and you find the message there. It goes further in a few cases:

  • Spark, Slack, and Messages, with Karui for Mac: the Mac sees the same notification and sends your iPhone a link to the exact email, message, or conversation. That only works if the Mac is on, awake, and running Karui for Mac when the notification arrives, so a Mac that’s always on, like a Mac mini, works best. If it was asleep or off, the tap opens the app.
  • Messages and WhatsApp, without a Mac: Karui opens the conversation when it knows the sender’s phone number or email address, from the notification or a VIP’s contact card. WhatsApp group chats open the app.
  • Every other app: tapping opens the app. To go straight to the message, tap the app’s original notification in Notification Center instead. Your Focus silences it, but it’s still there.
