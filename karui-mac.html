Karui for Mac Beta

Makes Karui for iPhone even smarter

Karui on your Mac improves filtering and clears notifications on your iPhone you’ve already dealt with on your Mac.

Download for macOS (version 0.5.0)
Free and open source. Requires macOS 15 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac, Karui 0.1.9 or later on your iPhone (currently waiting for Apple’s review on the App Store), and the same iCloud account on both.

Karui on a MacBook Air

Karui for Mac improves spam filtering on your iPhone.

Notifications that you read on your Mac clear on your iPhone.

Setting up:

  • Open Karui for Mac. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, press + and choose Karui.
  • That’s it. Karui on the iPhone shows the Mac under Settings → Sorting & Alerts → Karui for Mac.

Privacy:

Everything goes through the private part of your iCloud account, which only your devices can read. The Mac sends a fingerprint of each notification, its link, and whether you’ve read it; never the notification’s text. With History on your Mac turned on, your iPhone sends Karui’s decisions and each notification’s text, end-to-end encrypted. If you turn on alerts for senders you’ve written to, the Mac sends hashes of those addresses, not the addresses. Links are deleted after a day; the Mac keeps its copy of History for a month.

Reading Notification Center’s database needs Full Disk Access. Only Messages, Spark, and Slack notifications are read; other apps’ are never decoded. That’s a lot to ask of an app, which is why this one is open source.

View the source code on GitHub.

How it works:

Karui for Mac watches Notification Center for Messages, Spark, and Slack. For each notification it finds the exact message or email in that app’s own database and sends your iPhone a link to it, with a fingerprint of the notification, through your iCloud account. Tap Karui’s alert on the iPhone and the right thread opens.

It also notices when you’ve read one on the Mac and tells your iPhone, which clears the alert there.

Once a day it looks at who you’ve written to in Spark and Messages and sends your iPhone hashes of those addresses, never the addresses themselves. With “Alert for senders you’ve written to” turned on, their notifications get through like a saved contact’s.

With History on your Mac turned on, your iPhone sends Karui’s decisions and each notification’s text, encrypted, so the Mac shows your History. Marking read, changing a category, or deleting on either device carries over to the other.

The Mac has to be on and logged in; it can be locked.
