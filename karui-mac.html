Karui for Mac Beta

Makes Karui for iPhone even smarter

Karui on your Mac improves filtering and clears notifications on your iPhone you’ve already dealt with on your Mac.

Download for macOS (version 0.4.0)
Free and open source. Requires macOS 15 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac, Karui 0.1.9 or later on your iPhone (currently waiting for Apple’s review on the App Store), and the same iCloud account on both.

What it does:

  • Karui for Mac improves filtering on your iPhone.
  • Notifications that you read on your Mac clear on your iPhone.
  • Unlock routing to the correct thread for Messages, Slack, and Spark notifications.

The Mac has to be on and logged in; it can be locked.

Privacy:

Everything goes through the private part of your iCloud account, which only your devices can read. The Mac sends a fingerprint of each notification, its link, and whether you’ve read it; never the notification’s text. With History on your Mac turned on, your iPhone sends Karui’s decisions, and for apps the Mac doesn’t get, the notification’s text, end-to-end encrypted. If you turn on alerts for senders you’ve written to, the Mac sends hashes of those addresses, not the addresses. Links are deleted after a day; the Mac keeps its copy of History for a month.

Reading Notification Center’s database needs Full Disk Access. Only Messages, Spark, and Slack notifications are read; other apps’ are never decoded. That’s a lot to ask of an app, which is why this one is open source.

View the source code on GitHub.

Setting up:

  • Open Karui for Mac. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, press + and choose Karui.
  • That’s it. Karui on the iPhone shows the Mac under Settings → Sorting & Alerts → Karui for Mac.
