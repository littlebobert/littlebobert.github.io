Karui for Mac Beta

Makes Karui for iPhone even smarter

Karui on your Mac improves filtering and knows what notifications you’ve already dealt with.

Download for macOS (version 0.2.0)
Free and open source. Requires macOS 15 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac, Karui on your iPhone, and the same iCloud account on both.

What it does:

  • Karui notifications on iPhone now open the correct thread in popular apps: Spark, Slack, and Messages.
  • Read it on the Mac, and the alert on your iPhone clears itself.

The Mac has to be on and logged in; it can be locked.

Privacy:

The Mac sends your iPhone a fingerprint of each notification, its link, and whether you’ve read it, through the private part of your iCloud account; never the notification’s text. Links are deleted after a day. Only Messages, Spark, and Slack notifications are read; other apps’ are never decoded.

Reading Notification Center’s database needs Full Disk Access. That’s a lot to ask of an app, which is why this one is open source.

View the source code on GitHub.

Setting up:

  • Open Karui for Mac. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, press + and choose Karui.
  • That’s it. Karui on the iPhone shows the Mac under Settings → Sorting & Alerts → Karui for Mac.
