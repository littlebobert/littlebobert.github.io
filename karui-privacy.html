Privacy Policy

Karui

Overview

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Karui classifies notifications privately on your iPhone. The app requires no account, has no Karui server or cloud service, and sends no analytics.

Data the app handles

  • Notification fields passed to Karui by the Shortcuts automations you create.
  • For selected VIP contacts, an on-device snapshot of name, email addresses, and phone numbers. Manual VIPs store the name you enter.
  • Upcoming calendar event title and time context, read on-device.
  • Your policy settings and local notification history.

On-device processing and storage

Classification uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models when available, with a deterministic local fallback. Notification, VIP, and calendar context is processed on-device. VIP data, settings, and history are stored locally; policy settings are saved in UserDefaults.

No collection, sharing, or tracking by the app

Karui does not transmit app data to us, collect it on a Karui server, share it with third parties, or track you across apps and websites. The app contains no analytics. Apple and source apps may separately process data under their own terms when their features or services are used.

Permissions

  • Contacts: used for contacts you choose as VIPs and their local matching snapshot.
  • Calendar: used to read upcoming event title and time context on-device for classification.
  • Notifications: used to deliver replacement notifications for important results. Original notifications remain in Notification Center under the Focus settings you configure.

You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Restricting a permission may limit its related feature.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove it through available app controls, reset settings or history where supported, or delete the app. You can remove VIPs in the app. Deleting Karui removes its local app data through the standard iOS process, subject to how iOS manages backups and system data.

Children and general use

Karui is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal data to us, we do not knowingly collect children’s personal data through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Karui changes. We will revise the date above and describe material changes clearly on this page or through an appropriate app or release notice.
