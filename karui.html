Karui

Silence spam notifications. See messages people unsend.

Apple’s Focus modes, made smarter.

Download for iPhone
Requires iOS 27 or later and an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, which Karui uses to filter notifications privately. Free, with no in-app purchases, subscription, or sign up.

Also:

  • Auto-replies to your VIPs in Messages, WhatsApp, LINE, and Messenger while you’re driving, asleep, or busy
  • Lets your VIPs through your Focus, from any app
  • Judges spam by the message, not the app it came from
  • Lets one-time codes through
  • Combines quick messages from one person into one notification
  • Keeps every app’s notifications in one searchable history
  • Translates notifications into your language

Privacy:

By default, Karui runs entirely on your iPhone, with no server and no analytics. If you turn on Second Opinion, some notifications are checked by Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. Details.
