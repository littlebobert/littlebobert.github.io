Karui

Silence spam notifications. Reveal unsent messages.

More powerful than Apple’s Focus modes. Works with apps like LINE and Facebook. A unified inbox for notifications for every app, even unsent messages.

Join the Beta (version 0.1.0, build 12)
Free during beta. No sign up needed. Runs privately and entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence.

Features:

  • Better spam filtering than Focus modes
  • View unsent messages
  • Automatically translate notifications into your native language

How it works:

Karui evaluates notifications privately, entirely on device, using your important contacts and calendar info, and decides whether to show them or hide them.

Privacy:

Karui runs privately on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. It uses an on-device model from Apple, so your private notifications never leave your device. Karui has no server, does not require an account, and sends zero analytics.

Requirements:

Requires an iPhone running iOS 26 or newer and Apple Intelligence enabled.

Limitations:

Tapping a Karui notification opens the original app, but does not jump directly to the message. Just open the original notification for that.
