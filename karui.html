Karui

Silence spam notifications. Reveal unsent messages.

Apple’s Focus modes, made smarter.

Join the Beta (version 0.1.0, build 27)
Free with no in-app purchases or subscription. No sign up needed. Runs privately and entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence.

Also:

  • Auto Reply answers your VIPs in Messages, WhatsApp, LINE, and Messenger while you’re driving, asleep, or busy
  • Your VIPs always get through your Focus, from any app
  • Spam is judged by the message, not the app it came from
  • Recognizes one-time codes and lets them through
  • Several quick messages from one person arrive as one
  • Every app’s notifications in one searchable history
  • Translates notifications into your language

Privacy:

Karui uses your contacts and calendar on your iPhone, and your notifications never leave it. No server, no account, no analytics.

Note:

Requires an iPhone with iOS 27 or later and Apple Intelligence. Tapping a Karui notification opens the app, not the specific message.
