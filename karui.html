Already keep notifications off?

You might be missing a friend changing plans or an important delivery. Turn them back on: Karui lets those through and keeps promos from the same apps silent.

Also:

  • Rings an alarm when someone you choose has an emergency, even when your device is silenced
  • Lets one-time codes through
  • Combines quick messages from one person into one notification
  • Auto-replies to your VIPs while you’re driving or busy
  • Translates notifications into your language

Privacy:

By default, Karui runs entirely on your device, with no server and no analytics. If you turn on Second Opinion, some notifications are checked by Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. Details.
