Kehai (気配)

Pick up where you left off.
Browse windows intelligently grouped by task, not by app. Quickly return to your last task after an interruption.
Download
Open source for macOS. Download currently opens GitHub.

What it does:

Kehai shows your open windows and Safari tabs in one searchable browser, intelligently grouped by project or task. Then returning to work starts with context, not app names.

Privacy:

You choose when AI grouping runs. Apps can be permanently excluded from capture and AI, and the source is open for inspection.

Why “Kehai”?

Kehai (気配) is the sense that something is present without being seen.
