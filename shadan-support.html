Shadan

Quick start

  1. Open the Shadan app and turn on Shadan in Safari’s Extensions settings.
  2. Open a site you browse, like x.com, click the Shadan button in Safari’s toolbar, and choose Always Allow on This Website.
  3. Right-click a photo or video and choose Hide with Shadan.
  4. Confirm the look-alikes Shadan finds. It learns from every answer.

Troubleshooting

The popup says “0 images checked”

  • Allow the site in Safari → Settings → Websites → Shadan, then reload the page.

A hidden image isn’t hidden after a reload

  • Reload the page again.
  • If that doesn’t help, turn Shadan off and on again in Safari → Settings → Extensions.

Shadan hid something it shouldn’t have

  • Click Show, then answer No to “Hide images like this?”. Shadan learns from your answer.

Images blur briefly while loading

  • This is normal. Shadan checks each new image before showing it, usually in well under a second.

Some images behind a login aren’t checked

  • Most sites, including X, serve their images publicly, so being logged in doesn’t matter. A few sites only serve images to logged-in visitors; Shadan can’t download those to check them, so they’re shown normally.

The review screen finds few or no look-alikes

  • Shadan looks for look-alikes among the images you’ve recently scrolled past, so right after installing it has little to compare against. Browse for a while, then open the Shadan popup and choose Review more.

Remove a topic

  • Open the Shadan popup from Safari’s toolbar, click Delete next to the topic, then click again to confirm.

Requirements and limitations

  • Requires macOS 15 or later, Safari, and a Mac with Apple silicon.
  • Works on X and the other sites you allow.
  • Videos are checked by their poster frame only.
  • Very small images, such as icons and avatars, are skipped.
  • Recognition is assistance, not a guarantee. Some images you’d rather not see may still get through.

Reporting bugs

In the Shadan app, choose About Shadan → Report a Bug…. This opens an email draft with an anonymous diagnostics log attached, which you can review before sending.
