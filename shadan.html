Shadan

Hide the memes you’re tired of seeing.

Right-click a meme you’re done with. Shadan hides it, along with every repost and look-alike, as you browse.

Download for macOS (version 0.1.10)
For Safari on Mac. No account needed.

Why Shadan:

  • One right-click. Hide a meme once and Shadan catches its reposts, crops and screenshots too.
  • Learns what you mean. Confirm a few look-alikes and it will, for example, recognize the same character in new outfits and scenes.
  • Blurs when unsure. Borderline images are blurred, not hidden, and one click tells Shadan if it got it right.
  • Works wherever you browse. X or any other site you allow in Safari.

Private by design:
