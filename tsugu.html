Tsugu (継ぐ)

Slack messages, translated
Translates and forwards Slack notifications to your phone while you’re away.
View on GitHub
An open-source OpenClaw plugin for macOS.

How it works:

Tsugu listens through Slack, checks that you’re away, translates Japanese when needed, and relays the message through iMessage.

Privacy:

OpenClaw can be configured to use local AI models, and iMessages are end-to-end encrypted.

Why “Tsugu”?

Tsugu means “to connect, or relay.”
