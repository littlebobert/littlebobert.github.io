Changelog

Umete

0.4.4

October 12, 2026
  • Umete no longer sends your contacts' names to AI matching, and its privacy screens now describe iCloud sync.

0.4.3

October 12, 2026
  • Umete now fills custom dropdowns like Delta's date of birth and gender, no longer repeats your phone number in a second phone field, and leaves fields asking for a phone's owner alone.

0.4.2

October 12, 2026
  • Umete no longer fills the same phone number into a second phone field, or a phone number into fields asking for the phone's owner.

0.4.1

October 12, 2026
  • Fixed filling hidden fields, such as alternate phone numbers behind tabs, and the extension failing to start on pages open during an update.

0.4.0

October 12, 2026
  • AI matching is now free by default, with no API key needed. Faster, cheaper matching with OpenAI's Decisions API.

0.3.0

October 12, 2026
  • Ume is now Umete. New website at umete.jp.

0.2.0

October 12, 2026
  • Added iCloud sync, saved contacts for doctors and emergency contacts, and an optional toolbar badge on pages Ume can fill.

0.1.15

August 29, 2026
  • Switch back to Xcode 26 so I can publish a working TestFlight.

0.1.14

August 15, 2026
  • Composes Full name from first and last, fills Country of residence from the saved address country, and shows English update notes when the app is in English.

0.1.13

August 15, 2026
  • Put Type first in Saved Data, prefills phone country codes from the Mac region, defaults OpenAI to gpt-5.6-sol with high reasoning, and centers the About window title.

0.1.12

August 15, 2026
  • Fixed Add… refocusing a finished phone or date draft, and kept bug-report email headers when the AI log is truncated.

0.1.11

August 15, 2026
  • Fixed a reinstall error when leftover saved answers could not be decrypted, and included diagnostics in About → Report a Bug.

0.1.10

August 13, 2026
  • Fixed adding and deleting saved answers in Settings, and made Debug Logs always available in the Safari extension.

0.1.9

August 13, 2026

0.1.8

August 13, 2026
  • Added automatic signed macOS updates and a Keychain migration that should stop the extra unlock prompt on Mac.

0.1.7

August 12, 2026

August 12, 2026

August 12, 2026
