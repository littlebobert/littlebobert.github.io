Umete (埋めて)

Autofill everything, privately.

An intelligent Safari form filler for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Download for macOS
Requires macOS 12 or later. Free, with no in-app purchases, subscription, or sign up.

What it does:

Complete a form once and choose “Remember my answers.” On forms later, open Umete and choose “Fill this form.” You can see, edit, or delete saved answers in the Umete app.

Also:

  • Fills text fields, menus, checkboxes, radio buttons, and dates, even split ones
  • Saves addresses and contacts, like your doctor and an emergency contact, and fills each part into the right field
  • Uses AI for fields it doesn’t recognize, free and with no API key needed
  • Syncs your answers between Mac, iPhone, and iPad through iCloud, encrypted
  • Optionally badges its toolbar icon on pages it can fill
  • Skips passwords, card details, and other sensitive fields

Privacy:

Umete has no account and no analytics. Saved answers are encrypted on your device and in your own iCloud, and are never sent to an AI. For fields it can’t match itself, Umete sends only descriptions of the fields, like their labels. Details.

Why “Umete”?

Umete (埋めて) is Japanese for “fill it in,” the thing you say to a form.
