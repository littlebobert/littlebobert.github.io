What it does:

After filling a few forms manually, Umete will remember your answers. Later, open Umete’s Safari extension and tap “Fill this form” to autofill everything saved.

Also:

  • Syncs across your devices. Encrypted.
  • Saves addresses and contacts too, like your doctor and an emergency contact.
  • AI based matching is free with no account sign up.
  • Skips passwords, card details, and other sensitive fields.

Privacy:

Umete has no account and no analytics. Saved answers are encrypted on your device and in your own iCloud, and are never sent to an AI. For fields Umete can’t match itself, Umete sends only descriptions of the fields, like their labels, to OpenAI. Details.

Umete (埋めて) is Japanese for “fill it in” (as in a form).
