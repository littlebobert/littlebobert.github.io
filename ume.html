How it works:

Complete a form once and choose “Remember my answers.” On a similar form later, open Ume in Safari and choose “Fill this form.” You can inspect, edit, or delete saved answers in the Ume app.

Privacy:

Ume reads eligible form controls only when you ask it to remember or fill a form. Saved answers are encrypted with AES-GCM, and the device-only key is kept in Apple Keychain. Ume collects no analytics and skips passwords, payment details, security codes, secrets, tokens, and Social Security numbers.

View the source code on GitHub.

Optional AI matching:

Local matching always runs first. If you add your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, Ume can ask that provider to match otherwise-unrecognized fields. It sends sanitized field descriptions—not your saved answers or the values currently entered in the form—and applies returned mappings locally.

Why “Ume”?

Ume (埋め) means “fill it in” in Japanese—a compact command based on umeru (埋める), “to fill in.”

Requirements:

Requires iOS or iPadOS 15 or newer, or macOS 12 or newer. Enable Ume in Safari Extensions and grant website access where needed. Optional AI matching requires your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key.
